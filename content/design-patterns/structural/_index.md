title: "Structural Patterns"
description: "Patterns for composing classes and objects into larger structures."
weight: 20
---

Structural patterns describe how objects are wired together: wrapping, adapting, sharing and composing. The GoF catalogue contains seven: Adapter, Bridge, Composite, Decorator, Facade, Flyweight and Proxy. Several of them look alike in code — a class that holds a reference to another object — but differ in *intent*. The [wrapper patterns comparison](/comparisons/adapter-decorator-proxy-facade) puts them side by side.
