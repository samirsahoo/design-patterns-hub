---

Behavioral patterns deal with algorithms and with how responsibility is shared between objects: who decides, who reacts, and how variation is plugged in. The GoF catalogue contains eleven. All eleven are published, each with a runnable Java 21 example. [Strategy vs State vs Template Method](/comparisons/strategy-state-template-method) and [Observer vs Mediator vs Event Bus](/comparisons/observer-mediator-event-bus) compare the ones most often confused.
