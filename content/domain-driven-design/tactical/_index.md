Tactical building blocks are modelling conventions. They are not GoF design patterns, although a few of them share names with patterns such as Factory. Each planned article explains the difference where names overlap. [DDD Fundamentals](/domain-driven-design/fundamentals#tactical-design-in-one-page) already gives a one-page overview with code.
